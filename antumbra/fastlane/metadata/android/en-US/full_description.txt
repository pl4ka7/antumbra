Antumbra is a private web browser that sends every page through the Tor network. Websites see a Tor relay instead of your IP address, your network sees encrypted traffic instead of the sites you visit, and nothing you do is stored on the phone.

IDENTITIES, NOT JUST TABS
• Keep Personal, Work and Research apart, or create your own identity spaces.
• Each identity has its own cookies, logins and Tor circuits, so sites cannot link them, even when you are signed in to two accounts on the same site.

READ, SAVE, SUMMARISE
• Reader view strips a page down to its text: no scripts, no trackers, adjustable size.
• Summarise any article with AI, sent through Tor.
• Save pages to a vault encrypted with a hardware-backed key. Nothing is ever synced.
• A built-in directory of verified onion addresses for BBC, The Guardian, ProPublica, Proton Mail, DuckDuckGo and more.

A SHIELD FOR EVERY SITE
• See trackers blocked on each page, the circuit it uses and its identity.
• Turn JavaScript off or request the desktop site per tab.
• Find in page, downloads through Tor, and Antumbra can be your default browser.

BUILT ON TOR
• Tor runs inside the app. No other app or account is needed.
• Every site gets its own Tor circuit, so sites cannot link your visits through a shared exit.
• See the circuit behind any page: guard, middle and exit relays with their countries.
• "New circuit for this site" and "New identity" whenever you need a fresh start.
• Fail-closed by design: if Tor drops, pages stop loading. Nothing falls back to a direct connection.

WORKS WHERE TOR IS BLOCKED
• Built-in Snowflake, obfs4 and meek bridges, refreshed over Tor.
• Automatic mode starts direct and switches to bridges by itself when the network blocks Tor.
• Add your own bridges, pick an exit country, or connect through an upstream proxy.

LEAVES NO TRACE
• Every tab is private. Tabs, cookies and history live in memory only.
• Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked. The keyboard runs in incognito mode.
• Auto-wipe when you leave the app or after a few minutes away.
• Optional fingerprint or screen-lock protection, a discreet launcher icon, and a Quick Settings tile that wipes everything in one tap.

HARDENED ENGINE
• Gecko engine with fingerprinting resistance, strict tracking protection and HTTPS-only mode.
• Three security levels, from full websites to JavaScript off everywhere.
• WebRTC, location, motion sensors, downloads and links to other apps are blocked, because each of them can reveal who you are.
• Onion sites are supported natively, and Antumbra offers the .onion version when a site has one.
• Private search through the onion services of DuckDuckGo, Brave Search and Ahmia.

ASK AI, PRIVATELY
• Chat with hundreds of models using your own OpenRouter key, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including your own server on an onion address.
• Requests travel through Tor, so the provider never sees your IP address. Chats are never saved.
• Zero data retention routing is on by default.

WALLETS AND OTHER APPS
• Antumbra can share its Tor connection with wallets and other apps that support a SOCKS5 proxy.
• Payment and WalletConnect links open only after you confirm, with a clear warning about what leaves Tor.

HONEST LIMITS
Antumbra does not look identical to Tor Browser, so a website can tell them apart. No tool protects you if you sign in to your accounts or share personal details. For high-risk situations, use Tor Browser or Tails.
